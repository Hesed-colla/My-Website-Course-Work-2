Frequently Asked Questions

What are neurodevelopmental disorders?

These are disabilities associated primarily with the functioning of the neurological system and brain. Examples include Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, and speech impairments.

How can I find local support groups?

You can visit our Resources page or consult with local pediatricians and community centers to find verified parent support networks.

How can schools accommodate physical disabilities?

Schools provide accommodations through physical modifications (like ramps), assistive technology, and specialized Individualized Education Programs (IEPs).
