Categories of Special Needs

Understanding the diverse spectrum of needs helps us build a more inclusive environment.

1. Neurodevelopmental Conditions

Neurodevelopmental conditions involve differences in the way the brain develops and processes information. This category includes:

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD): Affects social communication and behavior patterns.
  • ADHD: Characterized by patterns of inattention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity.
  • Dyslexia & Learning Disabilities: Impact reading, writing, or mathematical comprehension.

2. Physical & Sensory Disabilities

Physical and sensory challenges can impact mobility, stamina, or how a child interacts with their physical surroundings:

  • Cerebral Palsy: Affects movement, muscle tone, and posture.
  • Visual/Hearing Impairments: Requires tools like Braille, sign language, or assistive auditory devices.
  • Sensory Processing Issues: Difficulty managing sensory information like loud noises or bright lights.
